The Context

Youth4Work was my formative product home — where I grew from Product Manager to Senior Product Manager over nearly a decade, co-building the core assessment platform during the explosive growth of Indian EdTech and HRTech.

"Building for education means building for potential. Every feature we shipped affected someone's career trajectory."
02

The Assessment Platform

  • Scaled to serve 300+ enterprise clients evaluating over 10,000 candidates per month.
  • Enhanced platform scalability to support 2x traffic growth without performance degradation during high-demand recruitment drives.
  • Redesigned bulk and individual candidate reporting module — reduced report generation time by 40% and improved HR team usability scores.
  • Established product testing and feedback loops achieving 95% platform uptime and 15% reduction in quarterly bug count.
  • Drove 30% improvement in feature time-to-market through structured cross-functional collaboration.
03

EdTech Platform for Educators

Identified a gap: local teachers and institutions needed tools to teach online without technical expertise. Built a platform empowering 500+ educators to offer online courses and video tutorials — expanding into a new revenue vertical and increasing subscriptions by 25% in 6 months.

04

The Long Game

Nearly a decade at one company gave me something rare: the ability to see the full arc of a product — from messy early decisions to scaled enterprise outcomes. That perspective shapes every product decision I make today.
