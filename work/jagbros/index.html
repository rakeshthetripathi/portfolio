The Beginning

Every seasoned product leader has an origin story. Mine begins at JAGbros Consultants — fresh from my B.Sc. in Visual Communication, designing complex workflow interfaces for enterprise clients in a fast-paced consulting environment.

"Design taught me that clarity is kindness. Every confusing interface is a failure of empathy."
02

The Work

  • Designed user-centric interfaces simplifying high-dimensional enterprise workflows — improving usability by 30%.
  • Developed standardised design handoff documentation, reducing design-to-development errors by 40%.
  • Conducted in-depth domain research to drive data-backed design decisions, increasing product engagement by 18%.
  • Delivered 8 key product features across 4 industry projects within 6 months, meeting tight deadlines consistently.
  • Analysed post-launch performance metrics and iterated on designs, leading to a 20% increase in feature adoption rates.
03

What This Built In Me

My design background is my secret weapon as a Product Manager. While many PMs think in features and roadmaps, I think in user flows, visual hierarchy, and interaction patterns. This allows me to give sharper feedback to design teams, write more precise product specs, and catch UX problems before they reach users. JAGbros gave me the craft. Everything since has been applying that craft at scale.
