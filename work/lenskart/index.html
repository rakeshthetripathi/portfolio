The Context

Lenskart is India's leading omni-channel eyewear brand — a unique intersection of retail, healthcare, and technology. During my 2021–2024 tenure, I led multiple high-impact initiatives that transformed how customers experience eye care end to end.

"Prescription errors were not just a business problem — they were a health problem. Getting accuracy right was non-negotiable."
02

The Core Problem

A significant portion of product cancellations were tied to inaccurate prescriptions. Customers would order eyewear, receive it, and return it because the power was wrong — a systemic issue rooted in how prescriptions were captured and verified at the point of sale.

03

What I Built

Tele-Optometrist Flow

Designed and launched an end-to-end video consultation flow where customers verify prescriptions with certified optometrists regardless of geography. Prescription cancellations reduced by 78%; accuracy reached 95%.

Eye Power Bank (EPB) Application

Digitised eye power capture and automated WhatsApp prescription delivery. Achieved 75% adoption rate within the first 3 months of launch.

Store Locator Revamp

Led a full re-platforming from PHP to Java backend with React JS frontend, plus UX redesign focused on intent-driven search and booking. Online appointments grew from 15,000 to 47,000 per month — a 200% increase.

Queue Management System and Self-Check-In

Designed and deployed QMS improving lead capture from 45% to 66% (a 47% relative improvement).

04

The Outcome

A fundamentally better customer journey — from walking into a store to receiving the right eyewear the first time. Agile adoption cut development cycle times by 25%. Feature utilisation up 18%. Team delivery efficiency improved by 30%.
