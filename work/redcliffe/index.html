01

The Context

Redcliffe Labs is one of India's fastest-growing diagnostic platforms. When I joined as Senior PM in April 2024, the challenge was clear: strong fundamentals with untapped potential in new segments, AI capabilities, and operational efficiency.

"The goal was not just to grow numbers — it was to make diagnostics more intelligent, accessible, and trustworthy for every user."
02

The Problem Space

Users were booking tests but not understanding their results. Phlebotomists had clunky workflows. Sales teams lacked smart recommendations. Support was overwhelmed with repetitive calls that could have been self-served. Each friction point was both a pain and an opportunity.

03

Key Products I Built

Understand Your Report (AI Lab Intelligence Tool)

Built an AI-powered tool allowing users to upload Redcliffe Labs or third-party reports. The system extracts parameters, applies weighted scoring, generates a personalised Health Score, and surfaces a natural-language summary highlighting critical, borderline, and normal indicators — driving a 25% improvement in user retention.

Family and Friends Health Network

Extended the report intelligence model to cover family networks — enabling shared health monitoring, personalised lifestyle, diet, yoga and exercise nudges, and automated smart reminders based on each member's report data.

AI Doctor Auto-Connect

When critical markers are detected in a report, the system auto-suggests and facilitates instant specialist consultation booking for follow-up and prescriptions.

Diagnostic Recommendation Engine

Personalised test and package recommendations based on user health profiles — driving a 30% increase in test bookings and 25% improvement in sales CRM conversion rates.

Booking Journey Automation

Granular event tracking across every user touchpoint eliminated friction steps — resulting in a 20% increase in successful payment completions and 22% reduction in booking cancellations.

AI Lead Prioritisation

Automated lead-to-conversion call scoring enabling sales teams to focus on high-intent leads, improving outbound conversion rates significantly.

VIP In-App Chat Support

Conversational AI for premium users, cutting inbound support call volume by 35% and average resolution time by 40%.

Phlebotomist App UX Overhaul

Redesigned the field technician mobile application, boosting sample collection efficiency by 20% and user satisfaction scores by 15%.

CRM Overhaul — Sales, Support, Ops

Reduced operational inefficiencies by 30% and boosted cross-functional productivity across all three CRM pillars.

04

The Outcome

Within 8 months: 25% revenue growth in new segments, 30% increase in test bookings, 25+ product improvements shipped, and a culture of high-velocity decision-making firmly embedded across the organisation.

05

What I Learned

In HealthTech, trust is the product. Every AI feature, every UI change, every CRM optimisation must serve one goal: making users feel confident, informed, and cared for. When you get that right, the business metrics follow naturally.
