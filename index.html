How I work

The habits behind the numbers.

01

Start with the problem

Discovery and user research before features. I map the journey with real data, from MoEngage to analytics, before deciding what to build.

02

Write it down

Clear PRDs, epics and user stories, with scope, out-of-scope, stakeholder impact and a rollback plan for every release.

03

Plan the ripple

Every change touches engineering, QA, CRM and operations. I plan the upstream and downstream impact before anything ships.

04

Measure what matters

Agile delivery tied to metrics and KPIs. AI where it moves a number: report summaries, recommendations, smarter decisions.
